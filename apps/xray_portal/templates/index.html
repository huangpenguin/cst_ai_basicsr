2. File checks and pairing

Matching filenames suggest a pair; they do not prove alignment or that a reference image is suitable. A reviewer must inspect the images.

3. Review pairs and assign splits

Review image alignment, reference quality and capture information. Exclude unusable pairs before freezing a version.

Reviewer only

Do not place repeats or different conditions of the same workpiece in different splits.

Assign a continuous slice range

Applies to every capture condition in the selected group. Leave excluded slices between train, monitor and test blocks.

Previews use the same 0–65535 display range and are reduced to 512 pixels. Download the original TIFFs to check fine alignment or small defects; thumbnails alone cannot establish these.

Freezing creates an immutable version. It does not start training.

4. Approved dataset and cloud sync

Only the training version and provenance are synced. Original archives stay on company storage. Test images are excluded from the training archive.
